Noto Reminders Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Noto Reminders is a simple app that helps you remember things. This page explains, in plain English, what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Your reminders stay on your phone.
  • We do not have accounts, servers, ads, or tracking.
  • We never see, collect, or sell your information.

What the app stores

The reminders you create (the text, date, and time) are saved only on your phone, inside the app. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

Microphone and voice

If you tap the microphone to speak a reminder, the app asks for permission to use your microphone. Your voice is turned into text by the speech recognition service that is built into your Android phone, which is usually provided by Google. Depending on your phone's settings, that service may send your voice to Google to be converted to text. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, not by Noto. Noto does not record or keep your voice. It only keeps the text of the reminder you confirm.

You can decline the microphone permission and type your reminders instead.

Notifications and alarms

The app asks to show notifications and to schedule exact alarms so your reminders go off at the right time. It also starts again after your phone restarts so your reminders are not lost. These are used only to remind you, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or photos.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not share or sell any information, because we do not have any.

Children

Noto Reminders is intended for people age 13 and older, and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
